# Business-Analytics-Problem-Solving Projects

A collection of **Business Analytics case studies** focused on analyzing business data, identifying trends and patterns, and providing data-driven recommendations.

These projects cover different business areas such as **customer retention, retail sales, marketing, pricing, profitability, and inventory management**.

---

## 📁 Projects

### 1. 📦 Boxify Subscription Churn Analysis

**Business Area:** Customer Retention

Analyzed subscription customer data to understand **churn behavior, customer engagement, subscription patterns, and retention opportunities**.

**Key Focus:**

* Churn rate analysis
* Customer segmentation
* Customer engagement
* Subscription behavior
* Customer Lifetime Value
* Retention strategies

---

### 2. 👗 FashionWave – Retail Store Sales Analysis

**Business Area:** Retail & Sales

Analyzed retail store sales to understand **product performance, store performance, weekend sales, and the impact of discounts on profitability**.

**Business Objective:**
Increase weekend sales by **15%**.

**Key Focus:**

* Sales performance
* Product category analysis
* Weekend vs weekday sales
* Discount impact
* Store performance
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Business recommendations

---

### 3. 💄 GlowUp Marketing Campaign Analysis

**Business Area:** Marketing Analytics

Analyzed marketing campaign performance to understand **campaign effectiveness, customer engagement, conversions, and marketing ROI**.

**Key Focus:**

* Campaign performance
* Conversion analysis
* Customer engagement
* ROI analysis
* Channel performance
* Campaign comparison

---

### 4. 🍔 TastyBites – Product Pricing & Profitability

**Business Area:** Pricing & Profitability

Analyzed product pricing and sales data to understand **revenue, costs, profit margins, and product-level profitability**.

**Key Focus:**

* Revenue analysis
* Product profitability
* Cost analysis
* Profit margin
* Pricing performance
* Best and worst-performing products
* Pricing recommendations

---

### 5. 🧸 ToyJoy – Inventory Analysis

**Business Area:** Inventory & Operations

Analyzed inventory data to understand **stock levels, product demand, stock-outs, overstocking, and inventory performance**.

**Key Focus:**

* Inventory level analysis
* Product demand
* Stock-out analysis
* Overstock analysis
* Inventory performance
* Reorder recommendations

## 🎯 Purpose

These projects demonstrate how **business data can be transformed into meaningful insights and actionable recommendations** using data analysis and visualization.

The focus is on understanding the business problem, analyzing relevant KPIs, identifying trends and patterns, and supporting **data-driven decision making**.
